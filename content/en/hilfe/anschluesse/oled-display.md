The wiring diagram shows the OLED display connected to a senseBox MCU-S2 (2.1).

### Connect the component

1. Disconnect the senseBox from its power supply before plugging in the cable.
2. Connect the component to an **I²C port** on the senseBox using the sensor cable. The diagram uses the port at the top right, labelled **I²C**.
3. Check that the plugs are aligned correctly and fully seated. Do not force them into place.
4. Reconnect the senseBox to your computer.
