The HDC1080 measures **temperature and relative humidity**. The wiring diagram shows the sensor connected to a senseBox MCU-S2 (2.1).

### Connect the sensor

1. Disconnect the senseBox from its power supply before plugging in the cable.
2. Connect the HDC1080 to an **I²C port** on the senseBox using the four-wire sensor cable. The diagram uses the port at the top right, labelled **I²C**.
3. Check that the plugs are aligned correctly and fully seated. Do not force them into place.
4. Reconnect the senseBox to your computer.

### Take your first reading

1. Open [Initialise HDC1080](doc:sensoren/hdc1080-initialisieren) and add the block to your program. It enables the connections and sets up the sensor.
2. Then add [Measure temperature](doc:sensoren/temperatur-messen) or [Measure humidity](doc:sensoren/hdc1080-luftfeuchtigkeit-messen).
3. Alternatively, start with the complete [Read HDC1080 completely](doc:sensoren/hdc1080-vollstaendig-auslesen) example.
4. Transfer the program to the senseBox. Depending on the measurement blocks you chose, the console shows temperature in **°C**, relative humidity in **%**, or both.

### If no readings appear

- Check that the sensor cable is connected to an **I²C port** and both plugs are firmly seated. Disconnect the power before changing the wiring.
- Make sure the initialisation runs before the first reading.
- Use the same sensor name in the measurement blocks and the initialisation. The examples call the object `sensor`.
- Try the complete example and check the console for error messages.
